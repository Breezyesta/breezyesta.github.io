Jarvis HQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Jarvis HQ is a personal tool that runs on one computer and is used only by its owner, Jewell (TikTok @breezyesta), to send his own edited videos to his own TikTok drafts.

What we access

  • When the owner connects his TikTok account, Jarvis HQ receives a TikTok access token, his TikTok open ID and basic profile information (display name / username).
  • Jarvis HQ uses this only to upload the owner's own videos to his TikTok drafts (inbox) for him to review and post himself.

What we store and where

  • The access token and refresh token are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private settings file. They are not sent to, shared with, or sold to anyone.
  • No other TikTok users' data is collected, stored, or processed.

Sharing

  • Jarvis HQ does not share, sell, or rent any data to third parties.

Removing access

  • The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time inside Jarvis HQ, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Contact

  • Questions: Message @breezyesta on TikTok
