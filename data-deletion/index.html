Jarvis HQ — Data Deletion Instructions

Jarvis HQ is a personal tool used only by its owner (Instagram @_jewell1wright_, TikTok @breezyesta). It stores access tokens only on the owner's own computer and keeps no data about other people.

To delete all data Jarvis HQ holds for your account:

  1. Remove the app's access: on Instagram go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, and remove "Jarvis HQ"; on Facebook go to Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Or message @breezyesta on TikTok or Instagram and ask for deletion — everything is deleted within 7 days.
